# New York Style Pizza

## Baker's Percentages
- Hydration: **62%**
- Salt: **2%**
- Sugar: **2%**
- Oil: **3%**
- Starter (100%): **10%**

## Ingredients
For 1X 16 inch pizzas

- 162 grams water
- 28 grams starter
- 9.0 grams oil
- 5.7 grams salt
- 5.7 grams sugar
- 270 grams flour

For 2X 16 inch pizzas

- 324 grams water
- 57 grams starter
- 18 grams oil
- 11 grams salt
- 11 grams sugar
- 540 grams flour

## Instructions
1. **Mix:** Combine the **starter, water, salt, sugar, and oil** in a mixing bowl. Mix until the starter and salt are fully dispersed.
2. Add the **flour** and mix until most flour is incorporated.
3. **Hydrate:** Let stand for ~30 minutes, then perform set of stretch and folds and knead in any remaining flour into the dough.
4. **Bulk ferment:** Allow to ferment until dough has doubled in size. Ideally, in the first ~2-3 hours of bulk fermentation perform 2 mores sets of stretch and folds.
5. **Divide:** Divide dough into two equal balls.
6. **Cold ferment:** Place balls into fridge for 1-4 days until ready to bake and to develop flavor.
7. **Relax dough:** Remove dough from fridge and let come to room temperature.
8. **Shape:** Coat peel with semolina flour. Flour each side of dough. Push air out from center, leaving the edges untouched. Then shake off extra flour and stretch into final round before placing onto peel 
9. **Toppings:** Add desired sauce, cheese, and toppings.
10. **Bake:** Bake at **500–550°F (260–290°C)** on pizza steel preheated for ~1 hour. Cook for **7 minutes**.
11. **Cool:** Let cool for a few minutes before slicing.
