# Focaccia

## Baker's Percentages
- Hydration: **80%**
- Starter: **10%**
- Salt: **2%**

## Ingredients
For 9x13 pan

- 450 grams water
- 60 grams sourdough starter
- 12 grams salt
- 570 grams flour
- Olive oil

## Instructions
1. **Mix:** Combine the **starter, water, and salt** in a mixing bowl. Mix until the starter and salt are fully dispersed.
2. Add the **flour** and mix until most flour is incorporated.
3. **Hydrate:** Let stand for ~30 minutes, then perform set of stretch and folds and knead in any remaining flour into the dough.
4. **Bulk ferment:** Allow to ferment until dough has doubled in size. Ideally, in the first ~2-3 hours of bulk fermentation perform 2 mores sets of stretch and folds.
5. **Cold ferment:** Optionally place into fridge for up to 4 days until ready to bake and to develop flavor.
6. **Relax dough:** If refrigerated, remove dough from fridge and let come to room temperature.
7. **Transfer:** Generously oil bottom and sides of baking pan and transfer dough to pan. Stretch dough toward edges of pan and ensure bottom of dough is well coated with oil. 
8. **Proof:** Cover and let rise until the dough is very bubbly and puffy.
10. **Dimple:** Drizzle generously with olive oil. Using your fingertips, gently press deep dimples throughout the dough.
11. **Toppings:** Optionally add any desired toppings such as brine, salt, or herbs. 
11. **Bake:** Bake at **425°F (220°C)** for approximately **20–30 minutes**, until deeply golden brown.
12. **Cool:** Let cool for at least 10–15 minutes before slicing.
