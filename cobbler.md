# Any-Fruit Cobbler

**Dish:** 9×9-inch (23×23 cm)  
**Oven:** 190°C / 375°F  
**Bake:** 40–50 minutes  
**Serves:** 6–8

## Fruit Filling

- 750 g fruit, fresh or frozen
- 75–125 g granulated sugar, depending on fruit
- 12–24 g cornstarch, depending on juiciness
- 15 g lemon juice
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 1 g salt

## Fruit Guidelines

| Fruit | Sugar | Cornstarch | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Blueberries | 100 g | 16 g | Fresh or frozen |
| Strawberries | 75–100 g | 20–24 g | Cut into pieces |
| Raspberries | 75–100 g | 16–20 g | Very juicy |
| Blackberries | 75–100 g | 16–20 g | Fresh or frozen |
| Peaches | 75–100 g | 16 g | Slice into ~1 cm pieces |
| Cherries | 100–125 g | 20 g | Pit and halve |
| Apples | 75–100 g | 12–16 g | Slice thinly |
| Plums | 75–100 g | 16–20 g | Slice into wedges |
| Mixed berries | 75–100 g | 18–20 g | Fresh or frozen |

## Cobbler Topping

- 190 g all-purpose flour
- 67 g granulated sugar
- 8 g baking powder
- 3 g salt
- 85 g cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 120 g whole milk
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 12 g coarse sugar, optional

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **190°C / 375°F**. Lightly butter a 9×9-inch baking dish.

2. Combine the fruit, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt. Stir gently until evenly coated.

3. Spread the fruit mixture evenly in the baking dish.

4. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

5. Cut the cold butter into the flour mixture until coarse crumbs form, with some pea-sized pieces of butter remaining.

6. Stir in the milk and vanilla just until a soft dough forms. Do not overmix.

7. Drop spoonfuls of the dough over the fruit, leaving some gaps for steam and juices to escape. Sprinkle with coarse sugar if desired.

8. Bake for **40–50 minutes**, until the topping is golden brown and the fruit filling is bubbling.

9. Let cool for **15–20 minutes** before serving.
