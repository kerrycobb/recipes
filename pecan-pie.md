# Pecan Pie

## Ingredients
- 1 9-inch pie shell
- 225 g pecan halves
- 175 g brown sugar
- 70 g unsalted butter
- 125 g heavy cream
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 5 g salt
- ~150 grams (3 large) eggs

## Instructions
1. Combine the brown sugar, butter (cut into pieces), heavy cream, vanilla extract, and salt in a microwave-safe bowl.

2. Microwave for 2 minutes.

3. Add the eggs and stir until well combined.

4. Pour filling into pie shell.

5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for approximately 40–50 minutes. Internal temperature at center should reach  200–205°F (93–96°C). 

6. Cool pie for 2-3 hours before slicing.
