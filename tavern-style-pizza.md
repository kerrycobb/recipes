# Tavern Style Pizza Dough

## Baker's Percentages
- 50% Hydration
- 10% Starter
- 8% Fat
- 2% Salt
- 2% Sugar

## Ingredients
For 1X 14 inch pizzas, 323 grams dough 

- 90 grams water
- 20 grams starter
- 15 grams oil
- 4 grams salt
- 4 grams sugar
- 190 grams bread flour

For 2X 14 inch pizzas, 323 grams dough each 

- 180 grams water
- 40 grams starter
- 30 grams oil
- 8 grams salt
- 8 grams sugar
- 380 grams bread flour

## Directions 
1. **Mix:** Combine the **starter, water, salt, sugar, and oil** in a mixing bowl. Mix until the starter and salt are fully dispersed.
2. Add the **flour** and mix until most flour is incorporated.
3. **Hydrate:** Let stand for ~30 minutes, then perform set of stretch and folds and knead in any remaining flour into the dough.
4. **Knead:** Over the next in the next ~2-3 hours, perform 2 mores sets of stretch and folds separated by ~30 minutes.
5. **Divide:** Divide dough into equal balls.
6. **Cold ferment:** Place balls into fridge for 1-4 days until ready to bake and to develop flavor.
7. **Relax dough:** Remove dough from fridge and let stand for an hour until it has reached room temperature.
8. **Shape:** Roll dough into 14 inch rounds on a floured surface. 
9. **Dock** the entire surface of each round.
10. **Dry:** Place rounds onto parchment paper and transfer to fridge for 12-24 hours.
11. Coat pizza peal with semolina flour and place round with driest side down.
12. **Toppings:** Add desired sauce, cheese, and toppings.
13. **Bake:** Bake at **550°F** on pizza steel preheated for ~1 hour. Cook for **10 minutes**.
14. **Cool:** Let cool for a few minutes before slicing.
