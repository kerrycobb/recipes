# Tavern Style Pizza Dough

## Ingredients
For 2X 14 inch pizzas 

- 160 grams water
- 80 grams starter
- 30 grams oil
- 7.8 grams salt
- 7.8 grams sugar
- 360 grams bread flour

## Baker's Percentage
- 50% Hydration
- 20% Starter
- 8% Fat
- 2% Salt
- 2% Sugar

## Directions
1. Mix water, starter, salt, sugar, and oil until starter fully dissolved.
2. Add flour and bring together.
3. Let dough sit for ~30 minutes.
4. Periodically knead dough 3 times over the course of a couple of hours.
5. When dough has roughly doubled in size, divide into 260 gram balls and put in fridge until ready to use. Ideally 2-3 days.
6. Roll into 14 inch rounds on floured surface.
7. Dock the entire surface of each round.
8. Place onto parchment paper and transfer to fridge for 12-24 hours.
9. Coat pizza peal with semolina flour and place round with driest side down.
10. Add toppings and cook on pizza steel preheated in 550&deg;F oven for 1 hour.   
11. Cook for ~10 minutes.
