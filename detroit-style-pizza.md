# Detroit Style Pizza

## Baker's Percentage
- 75% Hydration
- 10% Starter
- 2% Salt

## Ingredients
For one 9x14 inch pizza. 

- 225 grams water
- 30 grams starter
- 6 grams salt
- 285 grams bread flour

## Directions 
1. **Mix:** Combine the **starter, water, and salt** in a mixing bowl. Mix until the starter and salt are fully dispersed.
2. Add the **flour** and mix until incorporated.
3. **Hydrate:** Let stand for ~30 minutes, then perform set of stretch and folds and knead any remaining flour into the dough.
4. **Knead:** Over the next in the next ~2-3 hours, perform 2 mores sets of stretch and folds separated by ~30 minutes.
6. **Cold ferment:** Place dough into fridge for 1-4 days until ready to bake and to develop flavor.
7. **Proof:** At least 8 hours before baking, transfer dough to buttered and oiled pan. Once dough has reached room temperature, stretch dough to edges of pan. When ready dough should be very bubbly.
8. **Par-bake** at 480&deg;F for 8 minutes.
9. **Toppings:** Add desired sauce, cheese, and toppings.
10. **Bake:** Bake at **480°F** for **~20 minutes**, until toppings begin to char.
11. **Cool:** Let cool for a few minutes before slicing.
