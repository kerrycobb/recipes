# Detroit Style Pizza

## Ingredients
For one 9x14 inch pizza. 

- 195 grams water
- 60 grams starter
- 6 grams salt
- 270 grams bread flour

## Baker's Percentage
- 75% Hydration
- 20% Starter
- 2% Salt

## Directions
1. Mix water, starter, and salt until starter fully dissolved.
2. Add flour and bring together.
3. Let dough sit for ~30 minutes.
4. Periodically stretch and fold dough 3 times over the course of a couple of hours.
5. When dough has roughly doubled in size put into fridge until ready to use. Ideally 3-4 days.
6. Transfer to buttered and oiled pan.
7. Let rise in pan until well proofed. Early in this process, once dough has relaxed, stretch dough to edge of pan.
7. Par-bake at 480&deg;F for 8 minutes. 
9. Add toppings and cook another ~20 minutes until toppings begin to char.
