# Buttermilk Starter Cake

## Ingredients

### Main Batter

*Percentages are based on the total flour weight.*

| Ingredient | Baker's % |
|---|---:|
| Flour | 100% |
| Starter | 30% |
| Buttermilk/yogurt | 30% |
| Butter | 10% |
| Brown sugar | 10% |
| Egg | 10% |
| Vanilla extract | 2% |
| Salt | 2% |

- **Hydration:** 60%
- **Fat:** 11%

> **Note:** The buttermilk/yogurt can be reduced slightly depending on the consistency of the batter.

### Icing

- 25 g butter, softened
- 60 g cream cheese, softened
- 100 g powdered sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Baking

1. Preheat oven to **325°F (165°C)**.
2. Bake for **35–40 minutes**.
3. **Rotate the pan after 20 minutes**.
4. Let stand for approximately **10 minutes** before serving to allow the melted butter to absorb.
5. Spread the icing over the cake before it is completely cooled.
